Buying guides

Compare OKEYHAUL product links

When two marketplace listings share a lead photo, compare the original seller listing, item ID, selected color, stock status and return window. Save the listing date so you know when to recheck the details. Ask for warehouse QC photos of the specific variant if one image set covers several options.

Use the OKEYHAUL size guide to compare fit

Convert centimetres and inches before comparing charts. Start with chest width and body length for tees, shoulder width and sleeve length for jackets, and the laid-flat waistband and inseam for trousers. For sneakers, match your foot length in centimetres to the seller’s measurement instructions.

Build an OKEYHAUL shipping estimate

Write down the item subtotal, domestic delivery, agent charges and international freight in the same currency. Compare the quote using the parcel’s packed dimensions and actual weight, with and without shoe boxes if offered. For the US, UK or EU, use the actual destination and check what the quote says about import charges.

Frequently asked questions

What is an OKEYHAUL spreadsheet?

An OKEYHAUL spreadsheet is a term people use for a browsable collection of product finds and seller links. The category links on this page open the CAFANS catalog.

What can OKEYHAUL QC photos tell me?

QC photos can show visible condition, colors and details of the photographed item. They cannot establish authenticity on their own.

How do I estimate international shipping?

Use the destination and packed dimensions to compare carrier quotes. Check whether the route bills by actual or dimensional weight and which charges the quote includes.

How do I pick a size when charts disagree?

Use the chart for the exact seller and variant. For clothing, compare its garment measurements with a piece you own that fits the way you want.
